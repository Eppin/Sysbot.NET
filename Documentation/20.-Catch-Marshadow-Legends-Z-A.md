# Legends: Z-A: Catch Marshadow

This guide covers catching Marshadow until a `StopCondition` is met (e.g., shiny, specific IVs, etc.).

## Prerequisites

- Have access to the Marshadow encounter
- Position yourself in front of Marshadow

## Setup

1. Stand directly in front of Marshadow
2. **Important**: If you start the routine and the battle does not begin, take one more step forward
3. Once positioned correctly, **save your game**
4. Start the bot

## Catch Rate Cheat

- If you select **Master Ball**: No cheat needed (100% catch rate)
- If you select **any other ball**: Enable the 100% catch rate cheat

## How It Works

The bot will:
1. Initiate the battle with Marshadow
2. Attempt to catch Marshadow with your selected ball
3. Check if the caught Marshadow in B1S1 matches your stop conditions
4. If not, reset and repeat the process

## Tips

- Make sure your positioning is correct before starting (battle should begin immediately)
- Use stop conditions to target specific Marshadow (shiny, nature, IVs, etc.)
- Master Ball guarantees a catch without needing cheats
