# Legends: Z-A: Catch Volcanion

This guide covers catching Volcanion until a `StopCondition` is met (e.g., shiny, specific IVs, etc.).

## Prerequisites

- Have access to the Volcanion encounter
- Position yourself in front of Volcanion

## Setup

1. Stand directly in front of Volcanion
2. **Important**: If you start the routine and the battle does not begin, take one more step forward
3. Once positioned correctly, **save your game**
4. Start the bot

## Catch Rate Cheat

- If you select **Master Ball**: No cheat needed (100% catch rate)
- If you select **any other ball**: Enable the 100% catch rate cheat

## How It Works

The bot will:
1. Initiate the battle with Volcanion
2. Attempt to catch Volcanion with your selected ball
3. Check if the caught Volcanion in B1S1 matches your stop conditions
4. If not, reset and repeat the process

## Tips

- Make sure your positioning is correct before starting (battle should begin immediately)
- Use stop conditions to target specific Volcanion (shiny, nature, IVs, etc.)
- Master Ball guarantees a catch without needing cheats
